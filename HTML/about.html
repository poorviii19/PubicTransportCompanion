About This Project

Why this project exists and where it’s heading

🚧 Problem

New users, students, and tourists struggle to understand city transport systems, routes, costs, and transfers.

💡 Solution

A rule-based, frontend-only web application that suggests simple, clear, and reliable public transport routes.

🧠 Why Frontend Only?

  • ✔ Fast and lightweight
  • ✔ Offline-friendly architecture
  • ✔ Beginner-friendly and easy to understand

👥 Who Benefits?

Students, elderly people, tourists, and new city residents who want a stress-free public transport experience.

🚀 Future Enhancements

  • Google Maps integration
  • Live traffic and congestion updates
  • Offline PWA support
  • Multilingual interface
